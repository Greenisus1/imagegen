IMAGEGEN

Pictures are drawn in your browser by puter.js - free, no API key, nothing to install. Puter does ask you to sign in with a free Puter account before it will draw. You make that account yourself at Puter; this app never sees your password. Saved images are stored on this Pi.

One step first: Puter needs you signed in before it can draw. A small Puter window pops up. Sign in there, then come back.
